## About YUZIO

YUZIO is an AI-native company exploring a more human relationship between people and intelligent systems.

We believe the value of AI is not to fill every saved minute with more work, but to take on complexity that technology can handle — and return **time, attention, mental space, and choice** to people.

> **智能有为而知止，人方从容而有余。**

We build capable intelligence that knows when to act, when to ask, and when to stay quiet.

---

## What we believe

**Capability with restraint.**  
Intelligence should be able to get things done, while remaining bounded by permission, context, and human intent.

**Human agency.**  
AI should expand people's choices, not quietly make those choices for them.

**Quiet intelligence.**  
The best AI experience does not always need to look or feel like AI. Sometimes the best outcome is simply having fewer things to worry about.

**Privacy and control.**  
Memory, permissions, automation, and personal data should remain understandable, controllable, and reversible.

**Margin matters.**  
Efficiency is useful when the space it creates is allowed to remain yours.

## Engineering at YUZIO

Our products are designed around a few enduring principles:

- AI should be able to **act**, not only answer.
- Important decisions should remain **human decisions**.
- Automation should be **observable and reversible**.
- Permissions and data access should follow **explicit boundaries**.
- Intelligence should reduce interaction cost rather than demand attention.
- Product architecture should preserve user agency as capability grows.

Some repositories in this organization may remain private while products are under active development. Public projects, specifications, and open-source work will appear here as they become ready.
